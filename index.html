What we trade

General trading is our primary activity. We source physical commodities in origin markets and supply them in volume to businesses.

Steel

Coils, sheet and semi-finished products.

HS 72

Wood veneer

Sliced and peeled sheets for veneering and plywood.

HS 4408

Coal

Bulk coal for industrial and energy buyers.

HS 2701

Timber

Wood in the rough and sawn wood.

HS 4403 / 4407

Pulses

Dried lentils, peas and beans.

HS 0713

From the supplier's yard to the buyer's warehouse.

Markets and process

We source from suppliers in Europe, Singapore and across Asia, and supply into the United Arab Emirates and onward to other countries.

  1. Source Agree quantity, specification and price with the overseas supplier.
  2. Contract Sign supply terms with the distributor or buyer.
  3. Supply Arrange shipment and delivery into the destination market.
