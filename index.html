About the company

A trading and investment company registered in Dubai

Shambhavi Global Trading FZCO buys goods overseas and supplies them into the United Arab Emirates and other countries — general trading conducted with distributors, general trade partners and overseas buyers across Europe, Singapore, the UAE and other Asian markets.

Alongside its trading activity, the company invests surplus profits and its own funds in commercial enterprises — listed securities, unlisted shares and debentures, and commercial property.
